🎙 Record your own letter sounds

Tap Record, say the sound (like "buh"), tap Stop. Silence is trimmed and volume is made equal automatically. Recorded sounds are used first; the computer voice is used only for letters not recorded.

Allow the microphone when your browser asks.

🎙 Blend sounds (clusters)

Record each one once, like the sound a teacher says (for example "shr" as in shrub). It is reused in every word that starts or ends with it. The whole word at the end is always spoken by the computer voice.

🎙 Special sounds

Each sound is saved only once. If it is also used somewhere else in the app, recording it here updates it there too.

Tune the sounds (teacher)

Press ▶ to listen. If a sound is wrong, type another spelling (e.g. fuh, ff, fur) and press ▶ again. Changes are saved on this computer.

Blend starts (str, tr, bl ...)

Endings (ip, ub, im ...)

Read the sentence

📷 Scan & Read: read your own pages

Paste text, or upload a Word (.docx) or text (.txt) file. Text is split into pages of short sentences. Tap a word to hear it, a 🔊 to hear a sentence, or ▶ to read the whole page.
